The Apps Script Playground

Create accessible and sustainable systems from the spreadsheets you already have.

Paul Ivers · Digital Learning Specialist · Orono Public Schools

The premise

You can make an interactive web app today.

Not because everyone suddenly learned to code. Because you can now describe the tool you need, in plain language, and have it working the same afternoon. We aren't building the car anymore. We're driving it. And like any driver, we still have to learn the rules of the road.

The spreadsheet problem

Our systems are already built on many disconnected spreadsheets

Every school runs on them. Rosters, points, sign-ins, observations, and more forms than anyone can count. A spreadsheet is a fine place to keep data. It's a miserable place to ask people to work.

No guardrails

Nothing stops a bad entry or the wrong format.

No roles

Everyone sees everything, unless you play whack-a-mole with protected ranges.

One click from chaos

A stray paste or delete can wipe a whole column.

Wrong device

Never built for the person on a phone in a hallway.

Sick of protecting cells and ranges, or sharing more data than people need? Stop asking them to work in the sheet.

The reframe

A spreadsheet is your storage, not your app.

It already has tables, rows, and columns. What it's missing is a front door: an interface designed for the people using it, not a wall of cells. That front door is a web app.

Why now

Describe it. Let AI build it. Iterate.

The hard part was never the spreadsheet. It was building the app on top, and that used to take a developer. Now you explain what you need, in plain language, with a screenshot of your sheet. If the first version isn't right, you say so, and it gets fixed.

Once you're rolling

Built for district trust

Pick your access level
Just you, your whole organization, or anyone with the link. Google login decides who gets in.
Role-based views
Add a Roles tab to your sheet so students, teachers, and admins each see only their part of the app.
Two-way configuration
An admin panel can reshape the sheet itself; edits flow both directions.
Your data stays with you
Everything lives in your own Google Sheets under your existing district agreement. No new vendor, and no student data leaves Workspace.
Review before rollout
Anything touching student data goes through the same district review as any other tool. Builder-made doesn't mean unreviewed.
