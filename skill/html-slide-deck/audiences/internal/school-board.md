# Audience: internal / school-board

| Knob | Setting |
|---|---|
| **Tone** | Formal, measured, respectful of their time. |
| **Reading level / jargon** | Plain English. Spell out acronyms; no ed-tech jargon. |
| **Default theme** | Mode 1 (official), **light**. |
| **Density** | Low-to-medium — conclusions first, supporting detail tight. |
| **Formality** | High. |
| **`data-motion`** | **`calm`** — fades only, no travel; numbers settle without theatrics. |
| **QR / poll / badge** | **No QR, no polls by default.** A board deck is a closed-room decision document. |
| **Preferred close** | A clear recommendation and the specific ask / decision requested. |

## What to assume / emphasize
They decide; they don't operate. Lead with outcomes, cost, and impact. Make the
ask unmistakable. Avoid anything that reads as a sales pitch or a show.
