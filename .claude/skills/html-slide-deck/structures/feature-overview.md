# Structure: feature-overview

## Purpose
Explain **one feature** to people who already use the product. No need to sell
the whole thing — just orient them to what's new or under-used, show it, prove
the gain, and nudge them to try it.

## Default reveal mode
`manual` (often shown in a staff meeting). `autobuild` is a fine override for a
self-running version.

## Slide sequence
1. `cover`
2. `head-block` — what the feature is, in one breath
3. `demo-split` — see it work
4. `before-after` — life with vs. without it
5. `big-stat` — the gain (time saved, clicks removed)
6. `callout` — tips / gotchas
7. `recap`
8. `cta` or `close`

## Density guidance
Low to medium. Demo and before/after carry the message; keep the framing slides
to a sentence or two.

## Audience-overridable knobs
- `cta` vs `close` as the ending.
- Theme, tone, motion intensity — always the audience's.

## Polls / QR expectation
No polls by default. QR optional on the `cta` / `close` — a link to enable or
open the feature.
