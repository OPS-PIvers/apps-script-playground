# Structure: launch

## Purpose
Announce something new and make it land. Open big, prove the scale, name the
problem it solves, show it, validate it with a voice and a timeline, quantify
the impact, and ask for the next step. Built for momentum.

## Default reveal mode
`autobuild` — staged beats give the announcement its punch. Kiosk for a lobby
loop is a fine audience override.

## Slide sequence
1. `hero-announcement`
2. `big-stat` — the headline number
3. `comparison` or `head-block` — the problem it solves
4. `demo-split` or `two-up` — the features
5. `quote` — a voice that validates it
6. `timeline` — what happens when
7. `kpi-row` — the impact at a glance
8. `cta`
9. `close`

## Density guidance
Low and **dramatic**. One bold idea per slide, large stats, generous whitespace.
Let `big-stat` and `hero-announcement` dominate.

## Audience-overridable knobs
- `demo-split` vs `two-up` for the feature beat.
- Reveal mode (`autobuild` ↔ `kiosk`).
- Theme, tone, motion intensity — always the audience's (launch leans lively).

## Polls / QR expectation
No polls. QR welcome on the `cta` / `close` — pointing at the signup, waitlist,
or "learn more" destination.
