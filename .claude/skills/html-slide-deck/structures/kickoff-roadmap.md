# Structure: kickoff-roadmap

## Purpose
Launch a project with the team that will deliver it. Align everyone on the
goals, the scope, the timeline, who owns what, the risks, and the immediate next
steps. Forward-looking and operational.

## Default reveal mode
`manual` — a working meeting; pace to discussion.

## Slide sequence
1. `cover`
2. `head-block` — goals
3. `head-block` — scope (in vs. out)
4. `timeline` — phases and milestones
5. `people-grid` — roles and owners
6. `callout` — risks and unknowns
7. `checklist` — next steps
8. `close`

## Density guidance
Medium. The `timeline` and `people-grid` are the heart; let them be detailed.
Goals and scope stay to a few crisp lines each.

## Audience-overridable knobs
- Whether scope gets its own `head-block` or folds into goals.
- Theme, tone, motion intensity — always the audience's.

## Polls / QR expectation
No polls by default. QR optional — a link to the project hub, plan, or tracker.
