# Audience: internal / staff  (DEFAULT)

The everyday voice for Orono staff. This is the audience to assume when none is
named.

| Knob | Setting |
|---|---|
| **Tone** | Peer-to-peer, direct, collegial. |
| **Reading level / jargon** | Plain professional. District and ed-tech jargon is fine — they share the context. |
| **Default theme** | Mode 1 (official), **dark** (navy default). |
| **Density** | Medium. Staff can handle a full slide. |
| **Formality** | Semi-formal — a colleague talking to colleagues. |
| **`data-motion`** | `standard`. |
| **QR / poll / badge** | Off by default; turn on per need (a poll in a PD-style meeting, a QR to a shared file). |
| **Preferred close** | A clear "here's what to do next" + where to find it. |

## What to assume / emphasize
They know the district and the tools. Skip the background, get to the point and
the practical takeaway. Respect their time.
