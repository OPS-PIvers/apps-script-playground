# Audience: internal / district-leadership

| Knob | Setting |
|---|---|
| **Tone** | Strategic, confident, results-oriented. |
| **Reading level / jargon** | Professional. Ed-tech and operational terms are fine; tie them to outcomes. |
| **Default theme** | Mode 1 (official), **light**. |
| **Density** | Medium — metrics and trends, kept tight. |
| **Formality** | High-professional. |
| **`data-motion`** | `calm`-to-`standard`. |
| **QR / poll / badge** | Off by default. QR fine to link a fuller report. |
| **Preferred close** | The strategic takeaway + recommended direction or resourcing ask. |

## What to assume / emphasize
**Metrics-forward.** They think in initiatives, budgets, and district-wide
impact. Connect the work to goals and ROI. Anticipate "does this scale?" and
"what does it cost?"
