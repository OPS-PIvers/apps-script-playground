# Structure: workshop

## Purpose
Hands-on professional development. Set objectives, confirm the room is ready,
then move through the build step by step while learners follow along on their
own screens. Check understanding before moving on. Participation is the point.

## Default reveal mode
`manual` — you pace to the room, pausing while people do each step.

## Slide sequence
1. `cover`
2. `learning-objectives`
3. `agenda`
4. `prerequisites`
5. `statement` or `head-block` — the core concept
6. `step-screenshot` ×N — one per hands-on step
7. `try-it-now` — the do-it-yourself prompt
8. `live-poll` or `check-understanding`
9. `recap`
10. `close`

## Density guidance
Medium. `step-screenshot` slides can hold a real screenshot plus a short numbered
caption. Keep concept slides light so the work, not the slide, holds attention.

## Audience-overridable knobs
- Poll vs. `check-understanding` for the comprehension beat.
- Number of `step-screenshot` repeats (N).
- Theme, tone, motion intensity — always the audience's.

## Polls / QR expectation
**Polls ON** — this is the one structure where live participation is assumed.
QR optional (often used to hand out the working file or a feedback form).
