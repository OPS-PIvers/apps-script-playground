# Structure: info-session

## Purpose
A friendly general explainer for a non-expert audience — a family night, a
community meeting. Say plainly what the thing is, show it with big visuals,
explain what it means **for them**, answer the obvious questions, and open the
floor. Reassuring, never technical.

## Default reveal mode
`manual` — a presenter walks a room through it and pauses for questions.

## Slide sequence
1. `cover`
2. `statement` — "what this is"
3. `full-bleed` — big, friendly visuals
4. `head-block` — "what it means for you"
5. `feature-table` — used as an FAQ
6. `qa-prompt` — invite questions
7. `close`

## Density guidance
**Low and warm.** Big visuals, short plain-language lines, generous space. The
`feature-table` FAQ is the densest slide allowed and even it stays scannable.

## Audience-overridable knobs
- Whether `full-bleed` is one slide or a short run.
- Theme, tone, motion intensity — always the audience's (leans light, warm, calm-standard).

## Polls / QR expectation
No polls by default. QR welcome on the `close` — a link to "learn more," sign up,
or a contact.
