# Structure: briefing

## Purpose
Inform decision-makers — a school board or cabinet — and ask for a decision.
Lead with the executive summary, support it with metrics and a chart, weigh the
options, show the roadmap, then state a clear recommendation and the next steps.
Outcomes first, detail on request.

## Default reveal mode
`manual` — the presenter controls pace and stops for questions.

## Slide sequence
1. `cover`
2. `head-block` — executive summary
3. `kpi-row` — the numbers that matter
4. `chart-panel` — the trend behind them
5. `comparison` — the options weighed
6. `timeline` — the roadmap
7. `statement` — the recommendation
8. `checklist` — next steps / the ask
9. `close`

## Density guidance
Medium and **restrained**. Decision-makers want the conclusion up front and the
evidence tight. No demos, no flourish; a clean `chart-panel` does more than five
bullets.

## Audience-overridable knobs
- Whether `chart-panel` and `comparison` both appear or collapse to one.
- Theme, tone, motion intensity — always the audience's (briefings lean light + calm).

## Polls / QR expectation
**No QR, no polls by default.** A briefing is a closed-room decision document;
keep it self-contained.
