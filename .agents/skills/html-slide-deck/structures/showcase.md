# Structure: showcase

## Purpose
An external talk or keynote — the shape of the reference deck. Open with a
premise, name a problem the room feels, reframe it, then walk through proof:
concepts, a tour of cards, live demos, a before/after, and a take-home recipe.
This is the "stand on a stage and tell a story" structure.

## Default reveal mode
`autobuild` (presenter flips beats by hand; can run unattended). Kiosk is a fine
audience override for an expo booth or lobby loop.

## Slide sequence
1. `cover`
2. `statement` — the premise
3. `head-block` — the problem
4. `statement` — the reframe
5. `flow-diagram` — how it fits together
6. `section-divider`
7. `card-grid` — the tour of what exists
8. `demo-split` ×N — one per thing you show live
9. `before-after` — the payoff
10. `recipe-cards` — how the audience can do it too
11. `recap` or `checklist`
12. `close`

## Density guidance
Low per slide. One idea per slide, big type, demos carry the weight. Let the
`demo-split` blocks breathe; never crowd a stage slide with body copy.

## Audience-overridable knobs
- Reveal mode (`autobuild` ↔ `kiosk`).
- Number of `demo-split` repeats (N).
- Whether the persistent badge / QR rides along (see below).
- Theme, tone, motion intensity — always the audience's.

## Polls / QR expectation
QR and persistent slides/resources badge are **opt-in**, defaulting to ON for
external audiences (conference-peers, other-districts) and off for internal.
Live polls are not part of this structure.
